Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are

Trading Discipline Lab publishes short educational videos on YouTube (@thetradingdisciplinelab) and Instagram (@tradingdisciplinelabin). "Trading Discipline Lab Uploader" is our private tool that uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

What the Uploader accesses

The Uploader uses Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, only for the Trading Discipline Lab channel, to:

  • upload and schedule our own videos, and set their titles, descriptions, captions and playlists;
  • post comments on our own videos;
  • read our own channel's analytics (views, watch time, traffic sources) to improve future videos.

It does not access any other person's YouTube account or data. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers. Analytics are read as aggregate channel totals.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on our own computer and is used only by the Uploader. It is never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers

If you watch or comment on our videos, YouTube and Instagram handle your data under their own privacy policies. We only see what those platforms show every channel owner.
